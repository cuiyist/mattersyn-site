01
precursor preparation

Prepare AIS precursor mixture

Place 1.54 g ammonium citrate (reported as 8 mmol) in a 50 mL beaker. Add 15 mL deionized water, 87 µL (1 mmol) MPA and 0.25 g (0.84 mmol) InCl3·4H2O, then stir until completely dissolved. Adjust pH to 7 with 1 M NaOH. Add AgNO3 solution containing 0.03 g (0.16 mmol), followed by 0.38 g (1.60 mmol) Na2S·9H2O. The source reports a deep-brown solution upon full dissolution. Transfer to a cleaned 100 mL three-neck flask.
