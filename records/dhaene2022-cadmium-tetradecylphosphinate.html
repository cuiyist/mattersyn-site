01
precursor preparation

Precursor procedure step 1

CdO 642.1 mg, 5 mmol, 1 eq; acetonitrile 4 mL; TFAA 708 µL, 5 mmol, 1 eq; TFA 77 µL, 1 mmol, 0.2 eq. At 0 °C in ice bath stir CdO in acetonitrile; add TFAA and TFA; warm to RT overnight to clear/colorless solution. If white solid persists, a little more acetonitrile or heat may clear it.
