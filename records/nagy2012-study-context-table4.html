dispersion stability contextSource pp.2–3 describe DLS/aggregation and dark, cold storage: AUT dispersions remain optically clear without visible fallout despite the largest aggregates, with further-aggregation stability on the order of weeks; other ligand dispersions months. Exact storage temperature, durations and longitudinal DLS data are not given.reportedSource summary and interpretationnagy2012-study-context-table4-mua-3nagy2012nn204886b · Main PDF pp. 2–3 (printed pp. 4749–4750), Results: characterization and storage-stability continuation
sample designThe study uses shell-free CdSe QDs, nominal 3/5/10 nm core sizes, and four thiol-bound ligands. MUA/AUT are long C11 ligands; MPA C3 and CYST C2 are short. Charge, ligand length and size comparisons are source-study interpretations, not a universal material safety hierarchy.reportedSource summary and interpretationnagy2012-study-context-table4-mua-3nagy2012nn204886b · Main PDF p. 2 (printed p. 4749), Study design; p.7 Discussion
author interpretationAuthors tentatively rank surface charge, functionalization/ligand length and core size in their NHBE cytotoxicity study; positive QDs generally cause stronger responses. Cd/Se balance and AUT interaction, mitochondrial injury/uncoupling, and ROS-independent toxicity are proposed explanations, not independently measured composition, uptake or a proven unique mechanism. Smaller/long-ligand and concentration effects retain assay-specific exceptions.reportedSource summary and interpretationnagy2012-study-context-table4-mua-3nagy2012nn204886b · Main PDF p. 10 (printed p. 4757), Discussion pp.10–12
