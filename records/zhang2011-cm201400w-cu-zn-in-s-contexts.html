Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Preserve supplier wording and Methods wording separately. Printed mass and mole are internally inconsistent; neither is corrected.
  • Keep the Methods sequence exactly (initial 210 °C until clear, sulfur injection, selected 150/180/210/240 °C hold, then 230 °C for 5 min). Figure 3A first trace is 150 °C and agrees with Figures 1D, 2, and 4. The Methods do not explain the temperature transitions (including how the 150 °C hold follows the 210 °C preheat) or ramp rates; do not invent a monotonic or corrected sequence.
  • Actual SI labels HRTEM S4; S2 is In2S3 absorption/XRD. Bind to actual caption and preserve the mismatch.
  • TEM A–C are three explicit specimens; Figure1D and Figure2 show four temperatures including 150 °C. The three-sample prose does not explain the extra 150 °C XRD/EDS trace.
  • S5/Table S1 captions say “obtained under different reaction temperatures” but visible legend/columns are Cu/Zn ratios. Main text links these to composition series EDX; preserve caption mismatch.
  • The printed prose reports 620–750 nm for 2.0–7.0 nm particles. Figure 3B visually presents the same trend but its plotted endpoints do not map exactly to the prose range. Preserve this qualitative graph/prose discrepancy only; no digitized point values or new numeric rows are supported.
