Culture primary NHBE cells

Use 100 mm tissue-culture-treated dishes coated with 50 µg/mL type I rat-tail collagen and BEGM. Maintain humidified 37 °C and 5% CO2; feed twice weekly; passage by trypsinization. Experiments in triplicate using passages 3–7.
