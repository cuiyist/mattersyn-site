Synthesis protocolOrdered operations, material dependencies and stage-specific conditions

01
precursor preparation

Prepare Cs-oleate solution

Combine 1.2 g Cs2CO3, 3.75 mL OA and 45 mL ODE in a 100 mL three-neck flask. Hold under vacuum for 1 h at 120 °C, then heat under N2 to 150 °C until Cs2CO3 reacts with OA. Preheat stock to 100 °C before injection because it precipitates at room temperature.

02
precursor preparation

Prepare hot CuCl mixture

Load 10 mL ODE and 0.45 mmol CuCl into a 25 mL three-neck flask and dry under vacuum for 1 h at 120 °C. At 120 °C under N2 add 1 mL dried oleylamine and 1 mL dried OA; after complete CuCl dissolution, raise to 160 °C.

03
synthesis

Inject Cs-oleate and quench

Quickly inject 6 mL preheated Cs-oleate at 160 °C. Five seconds later cool the reaction mixture in an ice-water bath.

04
workup

Wash and redisperse hot-injection product

Centrifuge at 6000 rpm for 5 min; discard supernatant, add hexane and IPA to wash precipitate, centrifuge again at 6000 rpm for 5 min, then redisperse in IPA. Wash volumes and number of cycles are not stated.
