  • The 600 °C time series is distinct from the 24 h temperature series; no temperatures or time ticks are converted to invented individual tabulated measurements.

  • Text gives approximately 70 m²/g at 300 °C for both populations. Figure 7 B is visually higher; no plot-derived replacement is made.
