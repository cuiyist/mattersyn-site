Phase
Authors assign the approximately 0.32 nm local fringe as consistent with graphitic-carbon <002> spacing. No unit-cell parameters or measured coordinates are reported in the reviewed main article.
Morphology
Nanoscale dots; TEM diameter 1–4 nm and histogram reports ~55% near ~2 nm. Explicit quantified shape descriptor is not reported.

O/N functional groups reported by FTIR/XPS; zeta potential +30.2 mV.

The authors state that mass yield can reach 22.8%; isolated dry mass and batch mass are not separately provided.

The 100-fold diluted analytical solution (~0.19 mg/mL) is an assay aliquot, not a separate synthesis recipe.
