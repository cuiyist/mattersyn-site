Prepare nitride-window silicon wafer

On a 430 µm-thick, 100 mm Si(100) wafer, CVD-deposit 1 µm low-stress silicon-rich nitride. Align the window mask to (110), RIE the patterned nitride, KOH-etch exposed silicon through to back nitride, then protect both sides with 200 nm low-temperature CVD oxide. Source does not state coating/etch temperatures, KOH formulation, or times. The 100 µm observation window is part of the device, not particle size.

precursor preparation

Bond channel, glass via and nitride-window wafers

Anodically bond glass via wafer to the bottom of the Si channel wafer (Figure S1(c) marks −1 kV). Pattern 1 µm SU-8 2 (Microchem) on the window membrane side: source gives 1 min bake cycles without bake temperatures, 93 mJ/cm² I-line exposure, 30 s development, IPA rinse and gentle drying. Bake at 70 °C for 1 min then 90 °C for 5 min; align using Karl Suss BA6. Press stack on a 120 °C hot plate and manually roll a cylindrical metal weight for ~5 min; hard-bake in a 150 °C oven for 1 h. These are wafer-bonding temperatures, not nanocrystal reaction temperatures.

Silanize the cleaned oxide-coated channel walls with FDTS solution

After piranha cleaning, silanize the oxide-coated channel walls from a solution of 1H,1H,2H,2H-perfluorodecyltrichlorosilane (FDTS). Together with DDA in the reaction medium, this device-wall passivation prevents nanoparticle deposition. FDTS is not a measured nanoparticle ligand or an extra CdSe/Ag feed.

09
synthesis

Hydrodynamically focus CdSe between Ag+ streams and record Se K-edge XAS

Center nanocrystal stream is ~7 µm in the 20 µm-wide, 1.5 mm mixing channel; product is probed at channel positions. XAS uses Se K edge 12.50–12.86 keV. The 4 ms capability at faster flow is apparatus capacity, not a second reported specimen. Device context: 110 nL chip, three 20 µm inlets, 20 µm × 1.5 mm mixing channel, then 43 µm-wide × 403 µm-deep × 5.5 mm observation channel under a 100 µm nitride window. At 36 µL/min the center speed is 52 µm/ms; the 16 × 7 µm beam crosses at 45° and Se fluorescence reaches a seven-element Ge detector at 90°. ALS Beamline 10.3.2 supplies Se K-edge micro-XAS. The 4 ms resolution at 104 µm/ms is a device capacity, not this specimen's measured outcome.
