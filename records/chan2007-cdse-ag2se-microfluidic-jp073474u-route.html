03
synthesis

Hydrodynamically focus CdSe between Ag+ streams and record Se K-edge XAS

Center nanocrystal stream is ~7 µm in the 20 µm-wide, 1.5 mm mixing channel; product is probed at channel positions. XAS uses Se K edge 12.50–12.86 keV. The 4 ms capability at faster flow is apparatus capacity, not a second reported specimen.

Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Main Figure 4 stopped-flow caption says 6.67 mM AgClO4 before 1:1 mixing; Figure 5 says 6.66 mM. Both captions are retained; common 3.33 mM post-mix value is approximate.
  • Main p.4 body says both microfluidic XAS and stopped-flow observe exchange in 3.33 mM Ag+, whereas Figure 4 assigns the microfluidic trace 5 mM AgClO4 before side-stream mixing. Side-stream diffusion prevents a simple 1:1 chip dilution assignment; preserve both statements without merging conditions.
