Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Main p. 3 Figure 3 gives Au-Ps concentrations K × 10−9 M, CdTe 1.14 × 10−8 M, and nominal Au-Ps:CdTe ratios 0–3. The stated concentrations and listed ratios differ by about a factor of ten. Keep both as printed; do not repair concentrations or calculate mixing volumes.
  • Main p. 3 body prints CdTe concentration as 1.14 × 10− M with a missing exponent in the quenching example; Figure 3 caption and Methods give 1.14 × 10−8 M. Preserve the incomplete body notation separately.
  • Main p. 2 fluorescent-assay Methods specify a 1:1 Au-Ps:CdTe molar ratio, while Figure 5 states 1.12 × 10−9 M Au-Ps and 1.14 × 10−8 M CdTe. These are not numerically 1:1; do not resolve silently.
  • Methods describe the stated enzyme concentrations as dilutions before mixing 20 µL with 100 µL Au-Ps; Figures 4–5 label the assay curves with the same concentration list. The pre-mixing versus final-mixture concentration basis remains unresolved.
  • The CdCl2–GSH stock is labelled 1.25 mM CdCl2 and 1.05 mM GSH, alongside 1.2 mg CdCl2 and 1.6 mg GSH in 5 mL water. Preserve nominal labels and masses without substituting recalculated concentrations.
  • Main p. 4 text cites Figure 5B for the linear concentration relationship and Figure 5C for sensitivity comparison; actual Figure 5C is the linear fit. Retain the actual panel identities.
  • Centrifugation is described once and then as repeated three times; whether this means three total cycles or three repeats after the first is not made explicit.
