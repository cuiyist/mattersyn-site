Measure the source assay

Add proteins to QD solution in a quartz cuvette while stirring; measure fluorescence with a liquid-N2-cooled InGaAs OMA V detector, 15 cm focal-length SpectraPro 2150i monochromator and 632.8 nm HeNe excitation. Exact volumes, stirring speed and assay temperature are not reported. Figure 1a uses approximately 860 nM QDs with 0, 11, 22, 65 and 212 nM thrombin. Figure 1c separately compares 430 nM QD/178 nM thrombin and 860 nM QD/344 nM thrombin. Figure 1d uses approximately 430 nM QDs with 0 or 180 nM thrombin.
