Prepare CSe2 ethanol stock

Precipitate AgNCSe from equimolar KNCSe and AgNO3 in water, wash, vacuum-dry overnight; thermally decompose AgNCSe at 250–300 °C under vacuum and bubble its CSe2 vapor with nitrogen into ethanol. Absolute upstream charges and final stock volume are unreported.
