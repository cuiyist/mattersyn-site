Unresolved information

  • This is a core-only extraction of the standard 5 mL ethanol, single-loading silica route with the thin meso-P25 substrate preparation. Other variants remain pending with exact source locators.
  • Substrate dimensions, initial P25 stirring time, substrate spin-coating duration, dispensed precursor volume, 65 °C dwell and final CdO–silica calcination dwell are not reported in the corresponding detailed Methods paragraphs.
  • The 450 °C/1 h statement in the multiple-loading discussion is not silently transferred to the single-loading Methods route.
  • Nitric acid is named in the silica precursor description but its silica-stage charge is omitted from the detailed formulation. The separate 0.5 g, 65% acid charge belongs to the meso-P25 preparation.
  • No exact physical batch links join thin-substrate preparation to every microscopy specimen; Figure 4 III identifies the 5 mL formulation, while TEM size and fringes describe the broader silica-system sample context.
  • H2Se source/generation, gas quantity, chamber volume, numerical initial vacuum and a quantitative carrier-gas mixture are not reported. Source apparatus is a vacuum chamber designed for this reaction; this literature record is not a complete operating procedure.
  • The isolated CdSe yield, particle concentration and measured atomic coordinates are not reported. Solar-cell efficiency is outside the scoped as-prepared film product.

Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Silica-stage acid: Results p.4983 lists nitric acid in the infiltrated silica precursor; detailed silica formulation p.4988 lists no acid charge. Both statements remain visible, without borrowing the meso-P25 acid amount.
  • Comparator ethanol amount: Figure 4 caption p.4985 labels pattern II as 2.5 mL, whereas the synthesis formulation p.4988 lists alternative 3 mL. The primary 5 mL pattern III is unaffected; no comparator values are normalized.
  • Conversion atmosphere: detailed Methods p.4989 states chamber evacuation then about 100 Torr H2Se; Conclusions p.4988 summarizes N2/H2Se. No N2 pressure or mixture is inferred for the detailed conversion.
