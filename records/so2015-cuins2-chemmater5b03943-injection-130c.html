Composition
Cu-poor, indium-rich copper indium sulfide with zinc treatment · CIS is the nominal identity; elemental stoichiometry is source-measured only for selected temperatures.
Phase
Authors favor cubic zincblende; XRD alone cannot clearly distinguish cubic from tetragonal. Raman is described as disordered copper-deficient CuAu-type CIS. · Preferred source interpretation, not measured unit-cell coordinates; ZnS peaks overlap CIS. No definitive abrupt core–shell interface.

Not reported
Surface
Oleate-associated zinc-treated surface · FTIR supports replacement of TOP/OlAm by oleate; exact surface coordination unmeasured. Authors propose surface passivation for larger particles and more alloying for small particles.

Measurements are bound to the source-labelled injection-temperature population, not an individually tracked microscopic particle.
