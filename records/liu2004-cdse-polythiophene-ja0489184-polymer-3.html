Source distinctions and conflicts

  • SI p. 2 prints polymer 1 feed as 0.4 mg (~0.054 mmol) for polymer 3, while SI p. 3 reports 0.37 g product at 94% yield. The feed mass is retained literally, not repaired to 0.4 g.
  • SI p. 3 prints aqueous HCl as 0.5 mL of 2 M solution followed by 1 mL. Its actual addition volume is unresolved; neither alternative is silently selected.
  • SI p. 1 gives MALDI acceleration as 20 keV; SI Figure 2 caption gives 2000 V. Both source statements are retained.
  • Main Figure 1 labels 20 and 40 wt% CdSe; SI Figure 3 labels 5 and 12 vol%. No density conversion or panel-level physical-sample equivalence is asserted.
  • SI Figure 6 caption calls the plot open-circuit voltage, but its axes/title and SI p. 9 identify short-circuit current density. SI Figure 7 labels FF (%) while showing fractional-looking ordinate values; no conversion is applied.
  • Main p. 1 summarizes CdO and Se solution injection, while SI p. 6 explicitly loads CdO before injecting selenium in TOP. The detailed SI sequence is used for the operational recipe, with the main narrative retained as a source discrepancy.
