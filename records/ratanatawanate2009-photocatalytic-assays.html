Set up one catalyst–dye assay

In a 250 mL quartz round-bottom flask, combine 25 mg of one selected catalyst with 100 mL of one 25 ppm dye solution. Position 15 cm from the water-cooled 450 W Hanovia quartz mercury lamp in a dark box. Alternatives are separate assays, not combined charges.
