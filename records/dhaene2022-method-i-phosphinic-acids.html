precursor preparation

Precursor procedure step 4

For liquid n-hexyl: add stoichiometric KOH in 0.5 M EtOH, stir 1 h; concentrate and twice recrystallize from hot n-hexane/THF/EtOH (10/10/1 mL); slowly cool to RT, refrigerate, store overnight at about -20 °C; filter.
