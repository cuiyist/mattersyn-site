05
synthesis

Add alternating shell precursors

Inject calculated amounts of cadmium and sulfur stocks successively with an air-free syringe pump at 1 mL/h. Grow one layer at a time, leave 20 min between successive injections, and gradually increase the growth temperature from 180 to 240 °C for six monolayers. Individual aliquot volumes, per-layer temperatures and the complete time sequence are not given.
