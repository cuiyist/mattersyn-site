Synthesis protocolOrdered operations, material dependencies and stage-specific conditions

01
precursor preparation

Form and extract the metal-containing organic phase

Combine the aqueous solution of 0.21 g hydrated M(II) nitrate (either Cu or Ni) with 25 mL CHCl3 and 0.18 g sodium octanoate; stir vigorously. Discard the clear aqueous phase and retain the colored organic phase. The source describes this common precursor-preparation sequence for the NiS and Cu2S comparators.
