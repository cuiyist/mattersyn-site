Unresolved information

  • All three supplied main pages, Table I, Figures 1–3 and fourteen bibliographic entries were read; SI and cited full texts remain unreviewed.
  • Reflux temperatures are not reported individually. The conclusion states synthesis below 100 °C; this is a strict study-wide upper bound, not a measured temperature assigned to each solvent mixture.
  • Vessel geometry/capacity, stirring rate, heating ramp, atmosphere during reflux/annealing, centrifugation duration/RCF, washing volumes/repetitions, vacuum pressure, numeric drying temperature and product storage are unreported. Overnight drying is not converted to hours.
  • Scherrer crystallite sizes, approximate TEM aggregate diameter and BET surface area are separate quantities. No measured atomic coordinates, size distribution or raw curves are available.
  • Yield is source-calculated from precipitate mass with chemisorbed sulfate evaluated by TG-DTA; rows 11/12 contain dashes, not zero yields.
  • Run 12 Table I footnote states 600 °C for 1 h without ammonia washing; no ammonia step or 400 °C predecessor is inherited. Its exact physical parent specimen is unreported.
  • Runs 10–12 are supporting post-treatment comparisons using the row-3 preparation framework; they are not new independent growth routes or asserted physical replicates.
  • Source reports 6.6 wt% sulfate for H2O/EtOH = 250/0, outside the twelve tabulated runs; it is retained as unassigned contextual evidence rather than invented as run 5 or a complete new recipe.
  • Full molecular/crystal presentation remains pending. Generic molecule references and stage diagrams are explanatory, not measured solution or apparatus geometry.
  • Figure 2 caption labels ammonia-washed panel b as sample 11 and the 400 °C/1 h panel c as sample 12.
