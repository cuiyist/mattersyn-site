Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Main PDF p.3 explicitly says 15-min mixing at room temperature and p.7 says 45-min reduction at room temperature; main-referred SI PDF p.1 Figure S1 marks heating (Δ) during both steps. Both reports are retained without selecting a reactor setpoint.
  • Figure 2 has three incompatible panel attributions: visible labels show (d) size and (e) SAED; p.4 body and caption call the size histogram (e), with caption assigning SAED to (d); p.5 body calls SAED (e). The visible panels support d=size/e=SAED, without treating the body as uniform.
  • Main PDF p.5 body assigns the ~32.57° XRD peak to (112), while Figure 3 plot/caption labels (122).
  • Main PDF p.7 body attributes Figure 6c to changing CQD concentration and a steady intensity decrease; Figure 6 caption calls it varying AgNO3 amount, the legend has only 50/100/200/400 µL, and plotted intensity is nonmonotonic. No dose–response or reagent assignment is inferred.
