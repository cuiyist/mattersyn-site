Prepare TOPO-capped CdSe nanocrystals

Heat CdO/TDPA/TOPO in a 100 mL Schlenk flask under Ar to clarity at 320 °C, cool to 280 °C, quickly inject Se in TOP, immediately cool to 150 °C, reheat to 250 °C at 10 °C per 10 min, grow 4 h; disperse in toluene and precipitate with methanol.
