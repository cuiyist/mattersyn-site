Deposit dispersion on a substrate

Spread a colloidal dispersion onto a substrate. TEM preparation uses a drop on carbon-coated copper grids, wicks excess solvent with filter paper and dries in air. The self-assembly discussion says slow carrier evaporation; exact panel-specific evaporation conditions are not supplied.
