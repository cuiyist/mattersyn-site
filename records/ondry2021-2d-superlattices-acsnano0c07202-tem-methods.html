03
characterization

Rotate, Fourier transform and integrate

ImageJ bilinear rotation; GMS3.0 crop to 2^n x2^n pixels and apply 2D Hanning window. Log FT modulus displayed with fire LUT. DiffTools radial/azimuthal integrations. Autocorrelations calculated from raw TEM or STEM-EDS maps; TEM normalized to first Γ–M ([112̅0]SL) peak, EDS to central peak excluding origin. ImageJ linecuts along Γ–M. These are data-analysis operations, not new synthesized samples.
