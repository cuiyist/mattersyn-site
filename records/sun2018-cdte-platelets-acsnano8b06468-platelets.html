CdTe quantum platelets: optimized 3:7 amines, 100 °C, 16 h

Tellurium Precursor for Nanocrystal Synthesis: Tris(dimethylamino)phosphine Telluride

Haochen Sun; Fudong Wang; William E. Buhro · 2018 · 10.1021/acsnano.8b06468 ↗

Recordsun2018-cdte-platelets-acsnano8b06468-platelets
Evidence typeliterature protocol
Reviewsource reviewed

Optimized 3:7 n-octylamine/oleylamine CdTe platelets at 100 °C for 16 h, two precursor stock preparations, aliquot workup, primary product observations and selected cited figures. Other branches remain pending.

Core synthesis scope · other variants pending

Only the published core scope is covered by the linked audit. Pending variants have not been independently audited or included in training.

  • Platelets in the 1:9 amine mixture and their thickness Source: Main PDF p.2, Figure 2c; Main PDF p.6, Synthesis of Bundled CdTe Quantum Platelets; SI PDF p.2, Figure S1c
  • Early-time and single-amine controls Source: Main PDF pp.2–3; SI PDF p.3, Figure S2; SI PDF p.4, Figure S3
  • 70 °C magic-size nanocluster branch Source: Main PDF p.3, Figures 3–4; Main PDF p.6, synthesis paragraph; SI PDF p.14
  • Catalyzed quantum-wire synthesis and dilute Te stock Source: Main PDF pp.3–4, Figures 5–6; Main PDF p.6; SI PDF pp.5–6 and 14
  • Transamination mechanisms and NMR reaction studies Source: Main PDF pp.4–5, Figure 7 and Schemes 1–3; SI PDF pp.7–10
  • Beam-sensitive HRTEM, full-range XRD and trace-emitter PL Source: SI PDF pp.11–13, Figures S10–S12; Main PDF p.6, Characterization
