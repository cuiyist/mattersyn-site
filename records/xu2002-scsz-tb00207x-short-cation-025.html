Source distinctions and conflicts

  • At 0.5 mol/L urea, main p. 1 says pure cubic for ≤0.5 mol/L, while the conclusion says <0.5 mol/L. Figure 2a also shows a small nonzero monoclinic point at 0.5 mol/L. Preserve the boundary uncertainty, without an exact digitized fraction.
  • The general method gives urea concentrations 0.1–1.5 mol/L; Figure 3 includes 0.5 mol/L cations at a cation:urea ratio of 1:4, which implies a value outside that general range. Retain the explicit cation setting and ratio without silently changing either statement.
