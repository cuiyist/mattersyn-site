precursor preparation

Ethyl phosphinate ester formation, work-up and isolation

In an argon-filled glovebox, charge diethyl chlorophosphite (3913.8 mg, 3.595 mL, 25 mmol, 1.00 equiv) and dry THF (50 mL) to a 250 mL Schlenk flask; transfer to the Schlenk line and cool to 0 °C. Add the separately prepared Grignard mixture by filtered cannula transfer, then stir overnight at 50 °C. Evaporate solvent; add concentrated HCl (5 mL) and water (75 mL), stir 2 h at room temperature, extract twice with DCM (75 mL each), dry over Na2SO4, concentrate, purify on silica with ethyl acetate and vacuum-dry overnight. The source reports ethyl (2-ethylhexyl)phosphinate ester at 2.5 g, 49%, or ethyl (oleyl)phosphinate ester at 1.6 g, 18.6%, for the corresponding substrate branch. These are alternative outcomes, not one combined run.

precursor preparation

Oleyl alcohol preparation from oleic acid

In a 1 L flask, LiAlH4 (10 g, 260 mmol, 3.5 equiv) is degassed three times and placed under argon. Add dry THF (300 mL), cool to 0 °C, and add dried oleic acid (21.48 g, 24.0 mL, 76 mmol, 1.0 equiv) dropwise. Warm to room temperature and stir 4 h. Cool to 0 °C before carefully adding diethyl ether (400 mL), water (10 mL), 15% NaOH solution (10 mL), then water (32 mL); stir another 15 min. Dry with MgSO4, filter over Celite, vacuum-dry and vacuum-distill. Source reports colorless viscous product, 17.5 g (86%). This is a separate intermediate preparation, not a nanocrystal batch.

precursor preparation

Oleyl mesylate preparation (separate derivative)

Charge oleyl alcohol (13.42 g, 15.51 mL, 50 mmol, 1.0 equiv) in a 250 mL Schlenk flask, degas three times and place under argon. Add dry DCM (100 mL) and dried triethylamine (5.57 g, 7.67 mL, 55 mmol, 1.1 equiv); cool to 0 °C. Add dry mesyl chloride (6.30 g, 4.26 mL, 55 mmol, 1.1 equiv) dropwise and stir 30 min; add water (50 mL) and stir another 30 min. Retain organic phase; extract aqueous phase twice with chloroform (50 mL each). Combine organic phases; wash with 25% K2CO3 solution (25 mL) plus water (50 mL), then brine (50 mL); dry with MgSO4 and vacuum-dry. Product: colorless viscous liquid, 17.2 g (99%). This derivative is not linked as input to the oleyl-bromide route.
