Unresolved information

  • Complete supplied three-page main and identity-matched eleven-page SI, including all figures, crystallographic tables and numbered references/experimental notes. Cited full sources unreviewed.
  • Absolute Bi2O3/TiO2 charges, firing duration/ramp/atmosphere and pellet pressure are unreported. This upstream bulk preparation remains supporting context.
  • Lithiation is described as 3-mol excess n-BuLi for at least three days: no absolute n-BuLi or BTO charge, hexane volume, solution concentration, reaction temperature or atmosphere is supplied. Vacuum-drying conditions are unknown.
  • Water addition volume, exfoliation contact time/temperature, wash volumes/count beyond several, ultrasonic frequency/power and suspension concentration are not supplied. The centrifugation speed is rpm, not relative centrifugal force.

  • All SI atomic positions, cell metrics and bond lengths refer to the bulk Li2Bi4Ti3O12 intermediate, not measured coordinates of the exfoliated nanoplatelets or deposited film. No CIF or finite-particle model is claimed in this delivery.
  • AFM profile/height values, HRTEM flake thickness and EPD film thickness are separate specimens/scales. Resin section thickness is an analytical cutting setting, not platelet thickness.
  • SI Figures S3a/b are nearly black in the supplied PDF render. Source captions/text support qualitative film description and approximate thickness; contours, porosity and new measurements cannot be recovered from these images.
