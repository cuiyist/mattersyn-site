Composite Counter Electrode Based on Nanoparticulate PbS and Carbon Black: Towards Quantum Dot-Sensitized Solar Cells with Both High Efficiency and Stability

Yueyong Yang, Lifeng Zhu, Huicheng Sun, Xiaoming Huang, Yanhong Luo, Dongmei Li, and Qingbo Meng · 2012 · 10.1021/am301787q ↗

Two linked source-reported preparations: required solvothermal PbS powder and primary Sample C PbS/carbon-black composite counter electrode, PbS:CB 10:1 by weight. Included product evidence comprises PbS powder phase/size, incoming CB particle-size context, Sample C film morphology/thickness, and the named Sample C symmetric-cell EIS context with 2 M Na2S plus 2 M S. Other ratios, devices, electrolyte variants and stability applications remain explicitly pending. Exact physical batches across preparations and characterization are not established.
