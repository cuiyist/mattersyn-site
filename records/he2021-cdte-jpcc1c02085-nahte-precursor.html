precursor preparation

Load the vented precursor tube

Place Te powder and NaBH4 in a 1.5 mL EP tube, add water, and insert a needle to keep the reaction open to the atmosphere while H2 is discharged. Needle size and mixing speed are unreported.

02
precursor preparation

React in the constant-temperature mixer

React at 60 °C for 40 min until black Te disappears, a white precipitate appears at the bottom, and the solution becomes light purple. No subsequent solid separation is stated.
