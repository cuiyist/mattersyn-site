Unresolved information

  • Complete three-page main and matched ten-page SI, all three main and seven SI figures and 28 numbered main references. Cited full sources unreviewed.
  • Addition order and rate among the four solutions, mixing time, temperature ramp, hot pressure, centrifuge speed/time, purification details and toluene volume are not reported. Natural cooling to room temperature is explicit.
  • Open-atmosphere and anaerobic glovebox solution preparation are separate source routes; the glovebox atmosphere identity is unknown. SI explicitly assigns the same hydrothermal treatment to both.
  • Acetic-acid wash is a separate post-treatment of carbonate-containing powder: 5% is printed without basis, wash volume/time or independently reported final particle size.
  • Yield 85–90% is reported for the method without atmosphere-specific allocation. No size selection was performed.
  • The 20 nm TEM value describes only the 36% regular-cube subset of air-prepared particles. The approximately 58% cube-like and 6% truncated populations are distinct observations, not three new preparations.
  • HRTEM Figure S2, FTIR Figure S5 (caption 21.5 nm), TGA/DSC and optical curves are not given an exact physical-batch crosswalk. Their support records do not inherit all direct-route conditions or produce extra pairs.
  • No measured atomic coordinates are supplied. Pseudocubic cell indexing and interpreted local distortions are not a measured atomic model; neutron/PFM investigations are described as ongoing.
  • Independent source comparison passed; molecular/particle/reference-cell presentation remains pending.

Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Main p.3 pairs excitation 390 nm with 2.43 eV and 510 nm with printed 3.18 nm. Wavelengths are retained; the inconsistent energy/unit parentheticals are not corrected or used as energy measurements.
  • SI p.2 calls energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy EXAFS and prints FTIR range 500 to 400 cm−1; Figure S5 spans a broader plotted range. The printed technique/range mismatch is retained, not silently repaired.
  • SI p.3 places step 0.02° and 20 s per step in its PL sentence; their technique assignment is unresolved. Figure 2a separately gives the XRD slow-scan settings.
  • Main p.2 assumes pseudocubic indexing because cubic and tetragonal polymorphs cannot be resolved by these XRD data; Raman supports local tetragonal distortions. Cubic particle morphology does not establish a uniquely refined cubic phase.
