01
surface exchange

Exchange using aqueous AUT emulsion

Do not redisperse the purified QD pellet in toluene. Add an AUT-in-water emulsion to the pellet and sonicate extensively for about 3 h. AUT amount, emulsion concentration, temperature and power are not reported.
