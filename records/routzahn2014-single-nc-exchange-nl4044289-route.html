04
synthesis

Add methanolic AgNO3 to CdSe/CdS colloid in sealed cuvette

Manually shake three times, stir 4 min and acquire UV-vis/PL after each 5 µL addition until CdSe/CdS excitonic signals vanish; then add 15 µL more AgNO3. Final point is source-defined 107% relative exchange. Initial nanocrystal charge, total titrant volume and exact reaction temperature are not reported.
