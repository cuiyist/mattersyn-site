Source distinctions and conflicts

  • The Experimental Section uses 150 mL of 0.1 M GaCl3, corresponding to 0.015 mol GaCl3 by calculation. The discussion instead writes an example as 0.015 mol of 2GaCl3. Preserve that ambiguous stoichiometric wording rather than silently doubling the stock concentration or charge.
  • The source reports yield above 95% on a GaCl3 basis and an approximately 86% theoretical yield on a Zn basis. Its detailed denominator calculation is not supplied; these are different reported bases, not interchangeable yields.
  • The authors describe the particles as nonagglomerative and spherical. The low-resolution original TEM image remains available for readers to assess; no new quantitative aggregation score is inferred.
