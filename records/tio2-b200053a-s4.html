Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Table 1 explicitly assigns S3 to 30 psi, correlated 121.5°C and TAP 10 min, and S4 to 60 psi, correlated 145°C and TAP 5 min. Figure 1 and Figure 7 plot keys instead print microwave 5 min at 30 PSI and 10 min at 60 PSI. Table-labeled canonical conditions are preserved alongside the contradictory plot keys; no guessed reconciliation.
  • Figure 1 caption assigns a=S1 and b=S2, while its plot key labels a=hydrothermally treated and b=colloidal TiO2. Panel-specific sample identity is therefore unresolved; the caption and key remain separate.
  • Figure 7 caption assigns a=S1, b=S4, c=S3, d=S2; its plot key instead labels a=microwave 5 min 30 PSI, b=microwave 10 min 60 PSI, c=colloidal TiO2, d=hydrothermally treated. No exact curve-to-recipe mapping is silently inferred.
  • Main p.4 prose identifies the microwave electron-diffraction specimen as S3, but Figure 6 caption identifies S4. Figure 6 is shared ambiguous microwave context and not evidence of a separately identified S3 or S4 physical batch.
  • Brookite reference is PDF 29-1360 in p.2 prose but PDF 26-1360 in Figure 5 caption. Both printed designations remain visible; no corrected reference number or measured crystallographic file is supplied.
  • Main p.4 compares 544 W microwave for 12 min with 1300 W convection for 15 h. Methods specify 630 ± 50 W nominal oven power at 80%, 2 min ramp and the Table 1 TAP times. The comparison is attributed source prose, not a substituted power setting or recomputed energy.
  • The p.3 XRD peak-intensity/defect-plane explanation is preserved as author interpretation. It is not used to infer an absolute defect density or crystallinity percentage.
