  • Core growth has a reported approximately 4 nm product at 315 °C for 7 min. The broader 3–6 nm family and 7–10 min interval do not define extra sample-specific experiments. Diameter measurement method, crystal polymorph, refined cell and atomic coordinates are unreported.
  • The core ODPA mass is printed as 0.6426 without a unit; the separately printed 1.921 mmol is preserved. The prepared Cd stock vial contains 1.065 g but the explicitly injected amount is 0.95 g.

  • Cleaning centrifugation force/time, final drying duration, several wash volumes/counts and precise ligand-exchange isolation details are unreported. The early insoluble hexane/acetone fraction is discarded, not the recovered nanocrystal product.

Source distinctions and conflicts

  • Main p.2 cites Figure S9 for the 85%/15% surface composition; the actual annotated spectrum is SI p.16 Figure S8.
  • Main p.2 reports a 25% quantum-yield decrease for core-shell chloride exchange; the particular SI p.7 experiment reports 24.7% before and 20.1% after. Scope/normalization are not silently reconciled.
  • SI p.3 heading calls bis(trimethylsilyl)sulfide (5), while the main labels the silylated phosphonate ester 5. Main note15 also literally refers to addition of 5. Identities are not silently substituted.
  • SI p.3 textual model 31P peak14.18 and Figure S7 label14.19 differ in final digit. Figure S13 labels26.06/16.60 while main rounds26.1/16.6. Source precision is retained.
