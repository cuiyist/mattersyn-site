01
precursor preparation

Reduce AgNO3 to heterogeneous Ag nanoseeds

Preheat 10 mL 0.5 mM CTAC at 30 °C for 10 min while stirring 500 rpm. Add 25 µL 0.1 M AgNO3, wait 20 s, then add 450 µL freshly made ice-cold 0.02 M NaBH4 quickly. Stir at 30 °C for 1 h to hydrolyze unreacted NaBH4. The source specifies a 20 mL glass vial and magnetic stir bar; after NaBH4 addition the solution immediately becomes brownish and then yellow.
