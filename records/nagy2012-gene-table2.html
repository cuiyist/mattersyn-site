Preamplify cDNA

Pool 96 TaqMan assays at final 0.2× each. Reaction total 10 µL: 5 µL 2× PreAmp master mix, 2.5 µL pooled assay mix (0.2×), 2.5 µL cDNA containing 125 ng. One cycle 95 °C/10 min, then 14 cycles 95 °C/15 s and 60 °C/4 min. Dilute product 1:5 with TE, store −20 °C.
