03
synthesis

Immerse cleaned slides, then spread QDs

First immerse the cleaned ITO glass slides back-to-back in the MilliQ-water-filled Langmuir–Blodgett trough. Slowly deposit about 20–30 µL of QD stock onto the water surface; wait about 15 min for solvent evaporation. The slides are already immersed before spreading and remain for subsequent compression and upward transfer.
